Last Updated: June 10, 2026

1. Platform Service Scope

nanocorn acts strictly as an information matching platform and professional business broker. We connect independent developers (Sellers) with accredited buyers and investors (Buyers) for the acquisition of software assets, codebases, extensions, and domains. nanocorn does NOT hold custody of, manage, route, collect, or disburse any transactional funds. All monetary transactions are settled directly via Escrow.com.

2. Compliance & Zero-Funds-Touch

In accordance with Taiwan’s Banking Act and Electronic Payment Institutions regulations, nanocorn operates strictly under a "Zero-Funds-Touch" policy. Any dispute regarding transaction balances, funding dispatches, or verification is the sole responsibility of Escrow.com and the respective transacting parties.

3. Platform Broker Fee

nanocorn charges a flat **5% platform broker fee** on all closed M&A transactions. This fee is automatically deducted from the Seller's payout during Escrow.com's final settlement.

4. User Identity Sharing (KYC/KYB)

To facilitate secure and compliant Escrow transactions, users agree to share their legal names, verified email addresses, phone numbers, and receiving wire coordinates. These details are shared strictly with our transactional partner, Escrow.com, and are gated under prior legal consent.
