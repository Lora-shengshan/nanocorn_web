Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

1. Personal Information Collection

We collect personal information necessary for account provisioning and transaction escrow. This includes your name, verified business email, phone number, display name, and designated foreign-currency receiving bank wire coordinates.

2. GDPR, CCPA, and Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) Compliance

In accordance with the Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and CCPA, nanocorn strictly restricts un-consented personal data dissemination. Your real name, project URLs, and payment details are strictly encrypted inside the GCP Firestore database. They are revealed only to: - Verified Buyers who explicitly execute your product's Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). - Escrow.com once you check and submit the checkout Legal Consent form.

3. Storage & Portability

Your user profile, password hashes, and product metrics are stored locally inside an isolated, standalone GCP Firestore instance in the `us-central1` region. We do not store third-party Firebase tracking cookies or share logs with external advertising aggregators.
